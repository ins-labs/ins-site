# Ins brand kit (v2, unified 2026-10-19)

## The mark
The face-crescent: a female face in profile whose hair sweeps into a crescent.
Vector-traced from the avatar approved on 2026-10-15; the trace is the single
source of truth for every render. Never redraw by hand; always derive from the
master vector (master SVGs live in this repo under assets/brand/masters/).

## Colorways
- Primary (all external surfaces): lavender #e4dbf9 on navy #1e1b4a
- Site colorway: cream #f6f1e7 on green #0f4c3a
- Mono: #111 on #fff / #fff on #111 (fallback only)

## Rules
- One mark on every profile surface: chat apps, groups, Slack, GitHub, Google.
- Round-safe variant (mark at 88% height) for any surface that circle-crops.
- Small-size companion: crescent+star (favicon.svg) is used for 16/32px
  favicons only, where the face is illegible.
- Name: إِنس (never أنس); Arabic feminine forms always.
